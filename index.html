Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance? +
No booking necessary! Just walk in during our opening hours (11am - 7pm daily). We always have space available. You can also use our Quick Check-in to save time.
What about insurance? +
Items are stored at your own risk. While our storage area is secure, we recommend keeping valuables, passports, and important documents with you.
Can I store luggage for multiple days? +
Yes! The rate is CHF 10 per day per item. For longer stays, please contact us via WhatsApp to discuss arrangements.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cash (CHF) and major credit/debit cards. You can also pay online via our Quick Check-in.
What if I need to pick up outside opening hours? +
We can arrange early drop-off or late pick-up by special arrangement. WhatsApp us at +41 79 801 65 70 to coordinate.
